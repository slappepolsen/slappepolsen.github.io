App

SP Workshop

I made one of my childhood dreams come true: I made a free and open-source app! SP Workshop helps you work with subtitles for videos locally, on your own machine. You don't need to know complex tools or the command line. Just install it, open it, and click buttons.

  • Pull subtitles out of video files and translate them into another language
  • Burn-in subtitles back into the video so they permanently show on screen
  • Auto-transcription to draft subtitles from audio when none exist
